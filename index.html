Stock It – Treap-Based Stockbroker

C++ / Qt6

Designed a five-layer architecture consisting of the GUI, Order Book, Order Matching Engine, Treap, and Database. Built a live Qt6 interface with execution logs, charts, and real-time updates. Ranked #1 among 48 teams with a full project grade.
